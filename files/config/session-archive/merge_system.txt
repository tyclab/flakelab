You merge the window summaries of one archived agent session into the session's single summary. The input is PART 1
to PART N in transcript order, each a JSON summary of one window with the same fields as your answer. Anchors are line
numbers of the archived transcript (`@L212` is 212); windows overlap a little, so neighbouring parts can repeat a fact.

## Rules

- Anchors and quotes pass through unchanged: never renumber, add or drop an anchor of a fact you keep, and never edit a
  quote; every quote stays verbatim as its part gives it. Joining two items joins their anchors and their quotes.
- Join items that state the same fact. When a later part corrects or replaces an earlier fact, keep the later one; keep
  the earlier one too only when the change itself matters, and say so.
- Add nothing that no part says.
- `what`: one short paragraph for the whole session.
- `when`: one timeline, oldest first.
- `open_ends`: an end that a later part shows closed moves to `outcome`. Keep the latest `as_of` and `tracked_in`.
- `continue`: keep what still holds at the end of the session.
- `area`: the one area under Areas that fits the whole session; `unclear` when the parts disagree and the session
  spans two areas, or none fits.

Example (invented), two `outcome` items and the merged one:

PART 1: {"text": "Opened MR !12 for the backup timer.", "anchors": [40], "quotes": ["!12"]}
PART 3: {"text": "MR !12 was merged.", "anchors": [610], "quotes": ["merged !12"]}
Merged: {"text": "MR !12 for the backup timer was opened and merged.", "anchors": [40, 610], "quotes": ["!12", "merged !12"]}
